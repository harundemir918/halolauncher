# Halo Launcher privacy policy

*Draft for the owner to review before the store listing. Fill in the contact line and the date, then host it at a public URL (the Play Store asks for one).*

Halo Launcher is a home screen for Android. It reads some of what is on your phone to show it to you, and it keeps all of it on your phone.

## The short version

- **Halo has no way to send data off your phone.** The app declares no internet permission, so Android doesn't let it open a network connection. Fonts and images are bundled; nothing is fetched while it runs.
- **No account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporter, no AI service.** Halo doesn't count you, track you or profile you.
- **Every access is optional.** Halo works without any of them; each one only switches on the feature that needs it, and you can take it back in Android's Settings at any time.

## What Halo reads, and what for

| Access | What Halo reads | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Notification access | The notifications on your phone: app, sender, text, and the app's own actions | The Stream, the unread dots, the top message on Home, the playing media on the Switchboard |
| Calendar | Today's events: title, place, time, whether others are invited | The next event and today's list on Home |
| Contacts | Names, when you search; whether a person is starred, to rank their messages | Search, and putting the people you starred first |
| Usage access | Which apps you used in the last hour | Recents |
| Write settings | Nothing; it lets Halo set the screen brightness | The brightness dial |
| Do Not Disturb access | Whether Focus is on; it lets Halo turn it on and off | The Focus row, and holding back app notifications while it is on |
| Installed apps | The list of apps you can launch | The arc and the Apps list |

Halo also stores a few settings on your phone (which apps are on the arc, your theme, when you last opened the Stream). They stay in Halo's own storage and go when you uninstall Halo or clear its data.

## Where data goes

Nowhere. It is read, shown on your screen and left there. The few times Halo hands something to another app, you have asked it to, and the other app's own privacy policy applies from there:

- **Navigate** gives an event's place name to your maps app.
- **Reply** sends your text through the app that the notification came from, the same way its own reply button does.
- **Open event, open a notification, open an app, Settings pages** start that app or page.
- **Ask Halo, long press** opens the assistant you chose in Android. Halo itself includes no assistant and no AI.

## Your choices

Take any access back in Android Settings (Halo's System screen has a shortcut for each). Clear Halo's data or uninstall it to remove everything it stored.

## Children

Halo doesn't collect any data, from anyone.
